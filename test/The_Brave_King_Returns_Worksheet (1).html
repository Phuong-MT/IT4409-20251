SAY IT WITH A SUMMARY – PART 2

The Brave King Returns!
Retelling and Sequencing Worksheet (Grades 3–4)

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Recall key details and main events from a narrative text.
  • Arrange story events in the correct chronological order.
  • Retell a story briefly in their own words using complete sentences.

📖 Reading Passage: After the Dragon’s Defeat

After King Leo and Sir George defeated the dragon, peace returned to the kingdom. The people rebuilt their homes, planted new gardens, and celebrated their heroes with joy. One day, a messenger arrived with news — a small village near the mountains needed help. Their crops were failing, and they had no water. Without hesitation, King Leo sent Sir George to help. Together, they worked with the villagers to dig new wells and plant trees. When rain finally came, the fields turned green again. The villagers thanked King Leo and Sir George for their kindness and bravery. The kingdom once again flourished under their care, proving that true heroes protect not just with strength but also with compassion.

✏️ Part 1 – Sequence the Story

Directions: Number the sentences (1–6) below in the correct order.

  • ⬜ The people rebuilt their homes and celebrated their heroes.
  • ⬜ The villagers thanked King Leo and Sir George for their kindness.
  • ⬜ A messenger came with news that a nearby village needed help.
  • ⬜ King Leo sent Sir George to help the villagers.
  • ⬜ The heroes helped the villagers dig wells and plant trees.
  • ⬜ Rain came, and the fields became green again.

🌟 Teacher Tips

  • Review “The Brave King and His Loyal Knight” before beginning.

  • Use drawings or story cards for visual learners.
  • Optional: Let students illustrate their favorite part after writing.
